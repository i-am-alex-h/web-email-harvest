Examples of invalid email addresses

dot-atom does not permit a leading dot:
  .leadingDot@not.permitted.example (INVALID)

dot-atom does not allow commas:
  no,commas@not.allowed.example (INVALID)

The double quote character is not valid in dot-atom:
  not"valid@example.com (INVALID)

The double quote charatcer cannot be escaped in dot-atom:
  no\"escape@example.org (INVALID)
