Examples of ??? email addresses

The underscore character is permitted in domain in RFC 5322
since domain can be dot-atom but prohibited by RFC 5321:
  smtp@one.two_three.example.org (INVALID)
See https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5321#section-4.1.2

Double zero in domain-literal is permitted by RFC 5322.
RFC 5321 is unclear whether leading zeroes are allowed
in IPv4 addresses:
  James.Bond@[192.168.00.7] (INVALID)
See https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5321#section-4.1.2
